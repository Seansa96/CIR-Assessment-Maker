# Problem 8.1

## Key concept

Momentum is $p=mv$; kinetic energy is $K=\tfrac12mv^2$.

## Worked solution

1. Compute truck momentum: $p=(10{,}000\ \text{kg})(12.0\ \text{m/s})=1.20\times10^5\ \text{kg·m/s}$.
2. Equal momentum for the $2000\ \text{kg}$ SUV requires $v=p/m=60.0\ \text{m/s}$.
3. Equal kinetic energy requires $v=26.8\ \text{m/s}$.

## Why it works

Momentum is linear in speed, whereas kinetic energy is quadratic in speed.

## Issue signals

- `momentum-versus-kinetic-energy-confusion`
- `mass-speed-scaling-error`
