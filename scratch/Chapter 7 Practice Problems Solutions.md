# Problem 7.1

## Key concept

Near Earth, gravitational potential-energy change is $\Delta U_g=mg\Delta y$.

## Worked solution

3. Substitute the stated heights for the two requested changes.

# Problem 7.3

## Key concept

At rest, rope tension and the applied horizontal force balance the bag's weight in components.

## Worked solution

1. Use the rope length and sideways displacement to determine the rope angle.
2. Set $T\cos\theta=mg$ and $F=T\sin\theta$.
3. The rope does zero work because it remains perpendicular to its instantaneous displacement; the worker's work equals the increase in gravitational potential energy.

## Why it works

The bag moves higher while being displaced sideways, so the worker supplies the potential-energy increase.

## Issue signals

- `tension-does-work-along-arc`
- `sine-cosine-swapped`
- `potential-energy-sign-error`
