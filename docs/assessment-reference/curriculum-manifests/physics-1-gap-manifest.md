# Physics 1 Curriculum Gap Fill Manifest

## Objective
To populate missing chapters (12-17) from OpenStax University Physics into the CIR Assessment Maker, specifically covering:
- Elasticity
- Gravitation
- Fluid Mechanics

## Areas and Topics

### Area: physics-properties-of-matter
- **Topic**: `physics-elasticity`
  - *Objectives*: Understand stress, strain, and Young's modulus.
  - *Seminal Problem*: Column/Rod under Compression.

### Area: physics-gravitation
- **Topic**: `physics-universal-gravitation`
  - *Objectives*: Apply Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation to find g on various planetary bodies.
  - *Seminal Problem*: Measuring g at the Earth's surface / On other planets.
- **Topic**: `physics-gravitational-potential-energy`
  - *Objectives*: Use energy conservation in gravitational fields.
  - *Seminal Problem*: Escape Velocity.
- **Topic**: `physics-satellite-orbits`
  - *Objectives*: Model circular orbital mechanics and apply Kepler's laws.
  - *Seminal Problems*: 
    - Satellite Orbit (Speed, Period, and Energy).
    - Kepler's Third Law Applied.

### Area: physics-fluid-mechanics
- **Topic**: `physics-fluid-statics`
  - *Objectives*: Calculate hydrostatic pressure and apply Pascal's principle.
  - *Seminal Problems*: 
    - Pressure vs. Depth / Dam Force.
    - Pascal's Principle (Hydraulic Jack).
- **Topic**: `physics-buoyancy`
  - *Objectives*: Apply Archimedes' Principle to floating and sinking objects.
  - *Seminal Problem*: Archimedes' Principle.
- **Topic**: `physics-fluid-dynamics`
  - *Objectives*: Apply the Equation of Continuity and Bernoulli's Equation.
  - *Seminal Problems*: 
    - Equation of Continuity (Narrowing Pipe).
    - Bernoulli's Equation (Venturi Effect).
