# Chapter 15: Oscillations

Source guide: OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* §§15.1--15.6 (CC BY 4.0).

## Curriculum outline

1. **Simple harmonic motion:** equilibrium, amplitude, phase, period, frequency,
   Hooke's law, and the calculus relations among position, velocity, and acceleration.
2. **Energy in SHM:** conservation of mechanical energy and the exchange between
   spring potential and kinetic energy.
3. **SHM and circular motion:** the projection of uniform circular motion,
   phase and quadrature.
4. **Pendulums:** small-angle approximation, restoring torque, and the period of
   a simple pendulum.
5. **Damped oscillations:** amplitude envelopes, energy loss, and qualitative
   under/critical/over-damping distinctions.
6. **Forced oscillations:** drive frequency, transient versus steady state,
   resonance, amplitude, and phase.

## Required derivation examples

Derive the mass-spring angular frequency from Newton's second law; derive constant
SHM energy; connect the circular projection to sine/cosine motion; and derive the
small-angle pendulum period. Damping and forcing lessons must explicitly state the
model assumptions rather than presenting resonance as an unqualified rule.

## Media requirements

Use original spring-mass, energy-bar, circular-projection, pendulum, decay-envelope,
and driven-response diagrams. Include position/velocity/acceleration graphs where
they clarify phase.
