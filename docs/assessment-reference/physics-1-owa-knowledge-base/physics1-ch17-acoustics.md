# Chapter 17: Sound and Acoustics

Source guide: OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* §§17.1--17.8 (CC BY 4.0).

## Curriculum outline

1. **Sound waves:** pressure/displacement descriptions, compressions, rarefactions,
   and frequency.
2. **Speed of sound:** medium dependence, temperature dependence in air, and the
   air-speed derivation.
3. **Sound intensity:** power per area, inverse-square spreading, intensity level,
   and decibels.
4. **Standing sound modes:** open/open and open/closed tubes, allowed modes and
   harmonic restrictions.
5. **Musical sources:** strings, air columns, fundamental frequency, harmonics,
   timbre, and resonance.
6. **Beats:** superposition and the absolute frequency difference.
7. **Doppler effect:** moving-source/observer sign conventions and interpretation.
8. **Shock waves:** supersonic sources, Mach number, and Mach-cone geometry.

## Required derivation examples

Derive the speed-of-sound model in air at the intended Physics I level; show the
normal-mode boundary-condition reasoning; derive beats from a trigonometric sum;
and derive/interpret the Doppler and Mach-angle relations.

## Media requirements

Use pressure/displacement views, sound rays, intensity spheres, tube-mode diagrams,
instrument schematics, beat envelopes, moving-source wavefronts, and Mach cones.
